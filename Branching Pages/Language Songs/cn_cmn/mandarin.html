实用词汇

Shíyòng Cíhuì / Useful Vocabulary
聽見 (Tīng jiàn / to hear) : The verb "to hear" or "to be able to hear." Used to ask if someone can perceive something.
開門 (kāi mén / open door) : Literally "to open the door."
放狗 (fàng gǒu / let out the dogs) : Literally "to let the dogs out." Used figuratively to describe a bad situation or persistent nuisance.
放電 (fàng diàn / release electricity) : A figurative expression meaning "to flirt" or "to hit on someone."
說了 (shuōle / said) : The past tense of the verb "to say" or "to speak."
對我 (duì wǒ / towards me) : The phrase "towards me" or "to me."
要說什麼 (yào shuō shénme / want to say what) : The question "what do you want to say?"
笑 (xiào / to laugh) : The verb "to laugh" or "to smile."
變大魔頭 (biàn dà mó tóu / change big devil head) : The phrase "to change into a big devil/demon." (Figurative for a massive, dark personality shift).
眼睛 (yǎnjīng / eyes) : The noun "eyes."
美麗 (měilì / beauty) : The noun "beauty."
唯一 (wéiyī / only) : The adjective or noun meaning "the only one" or "unique."
世界 (shìjiè / world) : The noun "world."
就算 (jiùsuàn / even if) : The conjunction "even if" or "even though."
走進 (zǒu jìn / walk in) : The verb "to walk into" or "to enter."
沒有意義 (méiyǒu yìyì / have no meaning) : The phrase "it's meaningless" or "it has no point."
在一起 (zài yīqǐ / be together) : The expression "to be together" (often referring to a relationship).
放棄 (fàngqì / give up) : The verb "to give up," "to abandon," or "to renounce."
秘密武器 (mìmì wǔqì / secret weapon) : The compound noun "secret weapon."
靠近 (kàojìn / approach) : The verb "to get close" or "to approach."
回應 (huíyīng / response) : The noun or verb meaning "response," "reaction," or "to respond."
感覺 (gǎnjué / feeling) : The noun "feeling" or "sense."
從頭 (cónɡtóu / from beginning) : The phrase "from the beginning" or "to start over."
叛變 (pànbiàn / revolt) : The verb "to revolt" or "to rebel."
崩潰 (bēngkuì / collapse) : The verb "to collapse," "to break down," or "to crumble."
甜心 (tiánxīn / sweet heart) : The term of endearment "sweetheart" or "darling."
