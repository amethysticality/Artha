Vocabulario Util

Useful Vocabulary
Piensas (pien-sas / you think) : Second person singular present tense of the verb "pensar" (to think).
Olvidado (ol-vi-da-do / forgotten) : Past participle of the verb "olvidar" (to forget).
Sabes (sa-bes / you know) : Second person singular present tense of the verb "saber" (to know facts/information).
Pensa'o (pen-sa-o / thought) : Colloquial short form of "pensado," past participle of "pensar."
Sin parar (sin pa-rar / non-stop) : The adverbial phrase "without stopping."
Siento (sien-to / I feel) : First person singular present tense of the verb "sentir" (to feel).
Espiral (es-pi-ral / spiral) : The noun "spiral."
Crecer (cre-cer / to grow) : The verb "to grow."
Quédate (qué-da-te / stay) : Imperative form of "quedarse" (to stay/remain), combined with the pronoun "te" (you).
Lléname (llé-na-me / fill me) : Imperative form of "llenar" (to fill), combined with the pronoun "me" (me).
Promesas (pro-me-sas / promises) : The noun "promises."
Dime (di-me / tell me) : Imperative form of "decir" (to tell), combined with the pronoun "me" (me).
Aún (a-ún / still) : The adverb "still" or "yet."
Señal (se-ñal / signal) : The noun "sign" or "signal."
Hacerme (ha-cer-me / to make me) : Infinitive verb "hacer" (to make) combined with the pronoun "me" (me).
Revivir (re-vi-vir / to revive) : The verb "to revive" or "to come back to life."
Caos (ca-os / chaos) : The noun "chaos."
Llévame (llé-va-me / take me) : Imperative form of "llevar" (to carry/take), combined with the pronoun "me" (me).
Lejos (le-jos / far) : The adverb "far" or "far away."
Prometo (pro-me-to / I promise) : First person singular present tense of the verb "prometer" (to promise).
Amarte (a-mar-te / to love you) : Infinitive verb "amar" (to love) combined with the pronoun "te" (you).
Quisiera (qui-sie-ra / I would like) : Imperfect subjunctive or conditional form of "querer" (to want/wish). Expresses a polite wish.
Saber (sa-ber / to know) : The infinitive verb "to know" (facts/information).
Verdad (ver-dad / truth) : The noun "truth." (Used here in the phrase "en verdad" meaning "truly/really").
Sientes (sien-tes / you feel) : Second person singular present tense of the verb "sentir" (to feel).
Conocer (co-no-cer / to know) : The infinitive verb "to know" (a person/place/concept).
Mente (men-te / mind) : The noun "mind."
