유용한 어휘

yuyonghan eohwi / Useful Vocabulary
내 것 (nae geot / mine) : The phrase "my thing" or "mine."
시간 (sigan / time) : The noun "time."
순진한 (sunjinhan / innocent) : Adjective meaning "innocent" or "naive."
내숭 (naesung / pretense) : The noun "pretense," "affected shyness," or "coquetry."
속아 (soga / fooled) : The verb stem for being "fooled" or "deceived."
우는 (uneun / crying) : Present participle of the verb "ulda" (to cry).
남자들 (namjadeul / men) : The plural noun "men" or "guys."
다른 (dareun / different) : The adjective "different" or "another."
매력 (maeryeok / charm) : The noun "charm" or "attraction."
흔들리고 (heundeulligo / shaking) : Present progressive of the verb "heundeullida" (to shake/be swayed).
용길 내 봐 (yonggil nae bwa / try to be brave) : Phrase meaning "try to take courage" or "be brave."
다가와 (dagawa / come closer) : The verb "to approach" or "come closer."
가질 수도 있잖아 (gajil sudo itjana / you can have) : Phrase meaning "you could have" or "you might possess."
늦은 밤 (neujeun bam / late night) : The phrase "late night."
혼자 (honja / alone) : The adverb "alone."
들어선 곳 (deureoseon got / place walked into) : The place or spot where one entered.
춤 추는 (chum chuneun / dancing) : Present participle phrase for "dancing."
사람들 (saramdeul / people) : The plural noun "people."
그 속에 (geu soge / in the middle) : The phrase "inside that" or "in the middle of them."
왠지 (waenji / somehow) : The adverb "somehow" or "for some reason."
끌리는 (kkeullineun / drawn to) : The adjective meaning "drawn to" or "attracted to."
갖고 싶어져 (gatgo sipeojyeo / want to have) : The phrase meaning "I start wanting to have (possess)."
자릴 비운 (jaril biun / left spot) : Phrase meaning "vacated one's seat/spot."
십분 (sipbun / ten minutes) : The noun "ten minutes."
지루했던 (jiruehaetdeon / boring) : Adjective describing something that "was boring."
순간이 (sungani / moment) : The noun "moment" or "instant."
달라졌어 (dallajyeosseo / changed) : Past tense meaning "it changed" or "it became different."
오래된 연인 (oraedoen yeonin / long-term lovers) : The phrase "long-time lovers/couple."
중요한 사실 (jungyohan sasil / important fact) : The phrase "important fact" or "important truth."
끌리는 것 (kkeullineun geot / the thing that draws) : Noun phrase meaning "the fact that (you) are drawn."
거울 (geoul / mirror) : The noun "mirror."
붉은색 (bulgeunsaek / red color) : The noun "red color."
립스틱 (lipseutik / lipstick) : The loanword "lipstick."
화장 (hwajang / makeup) : The noun "makeup."
