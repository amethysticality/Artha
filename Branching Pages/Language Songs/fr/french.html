Vocabulaire Utile

Useful Vocabulary
Disparaître (to disappear) : Common verb meaning to cease to be visible or to vanish.
Fiasco (fiasco) : Noun meaning a complete failure or disaster.
Tenir tête (to hold one's head) : Expresses opposition or refusal to yield to someone's authority or arguments.
Prendre l'eau (to take on water) : Figurative expression meaning to be in difficulty, to be suffering emotionally, or about to give up.
Mensonge (lie) : Noun referring to an untruthful statement made with the intent to deceive.
S'en vouloir (to blame oneself) : Pronominal verb meaning to feel regret or to blame oneself for a mistake or fault.
P'tit à petit (little by little) : Compound adverb meaning gradually or slowly (contraction of *petit à petit*).
Tant pis (too bad) : Expression used to express the acceptance of a troublesome fact or a certain fatalism.
Menti (lied) : Past participle of the verb **mentir** (to lie).
Couler (to flow/to pass) : Used here in the expression **Le temps a coulé**, meaning that time has passed or elapsed.
Fatidique (fateful) : Adjective referring to something linked to destiny or having serious, unavoidable consequences.
Mots doux (sweet words) : Compound noun referring to tender words, compliments, or flattery.
Blindé (armored) : Adjective meaning protected or fortified. Used here figuratively to mean the heart is immune or insensitive to pain.
Aveugle (blind) : Adjective or the verb aveugler (to blind). Used figuratively here: love prevents one from seeing the truth or reality.
Trompe (deceives/cheats) : Conjugated form of the verb **tromper** (to deceive, to cheat). Means to mislead or to be unfaithful.
Si seulement (if only) : Exclamatory expression that introduces a wish, a deep regret, or an unfulfilled condition.
