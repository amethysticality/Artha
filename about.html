What Is Upcoming?

     As we move forward with the development of this app, we want to include more interactive features, like synced lyrics, quizzes, and a more sophisticated way of sorting languages, songs, and lessons. This could be done by implementing search functionalities and tagging systems.

Inspiration for the Design of the Website

     Thinking about the implementation of this website had me think about the evolution of language itself, and specifically, slang. Especially in English, many of our modern slang terms come from Harlem ballroom culture, a cultural movement that often invovled underground clubs in New York and London, often ran by the fringes of society. To pay homage to them, I wanted to inspire my site off of early 2000s club websites and forums, hence the blacks and simplistic design, albeit with a few added touches.
